# Hi there, I'm Junyi 👋

My career has been a journey through both creative and technical fields, driven by an interest in solving creative challenges with technology. I have had the opportunity to work in various roles, from product development to AI/ML and software development, and my focus is on connecting traditional creative processes with modern technological innovations.

My work is focused on three areas:

1.  **AI/ML Research & Development:** My work in this area involves architecting and implementing advanced systems. This includes building **agentic workflows**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and **function calling** using various frameworks. I have hands-on experience in **fine-tuning** large models and am familiar with emerging standards like **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**.

2.  **Full-Stack Development:** I have designed and built over a dozen web applications from end-to-end. I have a strong focus on the front-end, crafting intuitive user interfaces with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Next.js**, and **Tailwind CSS**, supported by back-ends in **Node.js** and **Python**.

3.  **Computer Graphics Research & Development:** My research in computer graphics explores real-time, physically plausible simulation. This includes hands-on work with modern graphics APIs like **WebGL/Three.js** and **WebGPU**, and engineering cloth simulations from first principles.
